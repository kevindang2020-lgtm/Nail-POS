 cash or a permitted manual split), and submit the complete pay run to the Owner App for final approval. Every cent
 remains traceable from earnings through payout and technician confirmation.

20. Required Test Matrix
Financial tests
 • $100.00 → $50.00 / $50.00
 • $100.01 → $50.01 / $50.00 using deterministic remainder rule
 • $0.01 → $0.01 / $0.00
 • 25/75, 10/90, 100/0, 0/100 and custom-dollar split
 • Under-allocation blocked
 • Overpayment blocked
 • Already-paid cash tips not paid twice
 • Partial payout preserves outstanding balance

Concurrency/idempotency
 • Manager submits 10 times → one submission.
 • Owner approves repeatedly → one approval, one lock, one payout-plan materialization.
 • Cash acknowledgment retries → one acknowledgment.
 • Check issue retries → no accidental duplicate checks.
 • Stale manager edit after owner review → PAYROLL_VERSION_CONFLICT.

Security
 • Tenant A cannot access Tenant B payroll, statements, payouts, checks or cash handoffs.
 • Branch A manager cannot mutate Branch B contribution without scope.
 • Technician A cannot read Technician B payroll.
 • Receptionist cannot approve payroll or change payout split by default.
 • Manager cannot self-approve own payout.

Cash drawer
 • Payroll cash movement updates expected drawer balance.
 • Insufficient drawer cash blocks payout from that source.
 • Canceled/unhanded cash does not count as paid.

24. Permanent Product Rules
Earnings and payouts are different concepts.
Manager prepares; owner approves.
Owner approval is audited and versioned.
50% check / 50% cash must reconcile exactly.
Manual payout splits must reconcile exactly.
Cash is a recorded payroll method, never off-book compensation.
Cash must appear on pay statements and reports.
Check payments require traceable check records.
Cash payments require traceable handoff records.
Technician cash receipt should be confirmed.
A planned payout is not a paid payout.
A printed check is not the same as a cleared check.
Paid and unpaid must always reconcile.
Already-paid cash tips must not be paid twice.
Multi-branch work must not create duplicate paychecks.
Locked payroll must not be silently rewritten.
Refunds after lock create linked future adjustments.
Technicians can view only their own payroll.
